Privacy Policy

madiland.ai · last updated 31 July 2026

madiland.ai is an independent publishing studio. We create planners, journals, coloring books and novels, and we publish our own posts to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account using an internal scheduling tool.

Who this policy is for

This policy covers the internal application madiland-social-poster, which we use to schedule and publish our own social media content. The application is private. It is not offered to, installed by, or made available to any other person or business.

What data the application accesses

The application acts solely on behalf of the account owner, using permissions the owner has explicitly granted, and only for assets the owner controls:

  • Publishing posts, images and reels to our own Facebook Page
  • Publishing posts and reels to our own Instagram professional account
  • Posting a comment on our own published posts
  • Reading engagement and insight metrics for our own content

What data we do not collect

  • We do not collect, store or process personal data belonging to visitors, followers or any other person
  • We do not access private messages
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties
  • We do not use data for advertising or profiling
  • We do not operate on behalf of any other business or Page

What is stored, and where

The application stores only what it needs to avoid duplicate posts and to keep a record of its own activity: the scheduled captions we wrote ourselves, the identifiers of posts it has published, and timestamps. This is kept in a private repository controlled by the account owner. Access credentials are stored encrypted and are never published.

Data retention and deletion

Publishing records are kept only for as long as the campaign is active and may be deleted at any time by the account owner. Because we hold no third-party personal data, there is nothing for other individuals to request access to or deletion of. Access can be revoked at any moment from Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites, which immediately stops all activity by the application.

Children

Our books and our social media accounts are intended for a general adult audience. The application does not knowingly process any data relating to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with an updated date.
